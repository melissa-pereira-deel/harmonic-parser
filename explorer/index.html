    What the exploration turned up

    • Folded octave is the right default for a hint. Every chord is seven keys wide, so diagrams line up in a list and a given key always means the same note. Try G7 or Bb9 in both windows: the fitted voicing shows the hand shape, at up to twice the width.
    • Coloured keys beat dots below about 24 px. Dots need room to resolve. At readout size, colour alone carries the chord and the root pip carries the rest.
    • The root needs a mark of its own. Folded into one octave, G7 and Bdim share three keys. The pip is what tells them apart, and it works in one ink.
    • The parser has no accent colour yet. Green, amber and red already mean clear, ambiguous and uncertain. This page proposes a blue --tone; the room reuses its existing accent.
    • Two hearings hinge on one unmeasured number. Across 30 common progressions, the repo's 0.06 margin finds two hearings in 1. At 0.10 it is 6, and at 0.12 it is 20. Drag the slider under option 5 on Am F C G to see the cliff.
    • Most of the hand usually stays. In option 6, C to Am moves one finger. Drawn as two separate chords, that looks like a whole new shape.
    • Every keyboard is an invitation. The concept brief keeps invitations apart from reflection, so options 3 to 6 change what the harmony room tests. Each can run as a fourth room instead.
